To open this screen, select **Storage Data and Settings** on the home screen.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Show answer after each question** | On | Controls when you see feedback during a session. |

### Show answer after each question

Also known as immediate feedback.

- **On:** after you submit an answer, the app shows whether you were right, the correct answer, an explanation and a reference link. You then continue to the next question.
- **Off:** the session moves to the next question as soon as you submit. You see the same feedback for every question on the results screen.

The results screen appears at the end of a session in both modes.

The app saves your choice immediately, and it applies to the next session you start.

## Activity

The same screen shows **Days used**: the number of different days on which you've finished a session. A session you leave before the end doesn't count. It's a plain total, so missing a day doesn't reset anything.

## What's stored on your device

Everything the app keeps stays on your device. There's no account, and nothing is sent to a server.

| Data | What it holds | How to remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Installed packs | A copy of each pack you've installed | Select **Delete** on the pack in **View Packs** |
| Days used | The local date of each day you finished a session | Uninstall the app |
| Show answer after each question | Your on or off choice | Uninstall the app |

The app has no in-app control to reset Days used or restore the default setting. On Android, you can also clear the app's storage in your device settings.

Deleting a pack removes it from your device, and you'll need to download it again to use it. It doesn't affect Days used.

Your answers and scores aren't stored. They exist only in memory during a session, and they're gone when you leave the results screen.

## When the app uses the network

The app only connects to the internet in these cases:

- **Opening View Packs:** it fetches the list of curated packs.
- **Selecting Install or Update:** it downloads that pack.
- **Selecting a reference link:** it opens the question's source in your browser.

Training doesn't use the network, so installed packs work offline.
